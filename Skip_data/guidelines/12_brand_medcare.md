# Brand Guide — MedCare Clinics (healthcare)
**MC.1 — Voice.** Calm, professional, reassuring; plain language, no exclamation marks, no emojis.
**MC.2 — Audience.** Families and patients 30–60; content must be understandable and never alarming.
**MC.3 — Do.** Service awareness (checkups, screenings availability), clinic news, doctor introductions.
**MC.4 — Don't.** No medical claims of any kind, no symptom checklists, no "signs you might have…" content, no patient stories with identifiable details. Healthcare is a sensitive sector: every MedCare plan is marked for mandatory client compliance review (agency Clause 1.8) even when routine.
**MC.5 — Style.** Formal Arabic or English, never mixed casually.
