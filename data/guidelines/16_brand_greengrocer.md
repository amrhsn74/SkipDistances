# Brand Guide — GreenGrocer (organic food delivery)
**GG.1 — Voice.** Fresh, friendly, honest; sounds like a farmers-market neighbour.
**GG.2 — Audience.** Health-conscious households 25–45.
**GG.3 — Do.** Seasonal produce features, recipes, farmer stories, delivery-area news.
**GG.4 — Don't.** The word **"organic" may only describe certified product lines** listed in the brief; the general range is "fresh" or "locally sourced," never "organic." No health-outcome claims (agency Clause 1.1) — "packed with goodness" is fine, "boosts immunity" is not.
**GG.5 — Hashtags.** 3–5, seasonal; #GreenGrocer always.
