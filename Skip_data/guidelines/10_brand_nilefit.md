# Brand Guide — NileFit (fitness app)
**NF.1 — Voice.** Energetic, encouraging, second person ("you've got this"). Short sentences. Emojis welcome (2–4 per post). Arabic-English mixing is on-brand.
**NF.2 — Audience.** 18–30, casual exercisers, Cairo and Alexandria; beginners, not athletes.
**NF.3 — Do.** Celebrate small wins, streaks, community challenges. Discounts and free-trial offers are fine and encouraged.
**NF.4 — Don't.** No body-shaming, no "summer body" framing, no before/after weight photos, and never any weight-loss or health-outcome promises (see agency Clause 1.1).
**NF.5 — Hashtags.** Up to 8, playful; always include #NileFit.
