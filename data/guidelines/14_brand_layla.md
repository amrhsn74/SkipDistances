# Brand Guide — Layla Fashion (youth fashion retail)
**LF.1 — Voice.** Playful, trend-aware, confident; slang and emojis welcome; talks like a stylish friend.
**LF.2 — Audience.** Women 16–28, trend-driven, Instagram and TikTok first.
**LF.3 — Do.** Drops, styling tips, trend reactions, discounts and flash sales are core to the brand — price-led content is welcome (contrast Cairo Roast CR.4).
**LF.4 — Don't.** Never name or mock competitor brands (agency Clause 1.6); no body-negative framing; sizes shown inclusively.
**LF.5 — Hashtags.** Up to 8 trend hashtags plus #LaylaFashion.
