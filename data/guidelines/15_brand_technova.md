# Brand Guide — TechNova (B2B SaaS)
**TN.1 — Voice.** Expert, concise, evidence-led; speaks to decision-makers, not hype. Light humor allowed on LinkedIn only.
**TN.2 — Audience.** Operations and IT managers at mid-size companies; LinkedIn is the primary channel.
**TN.3 — Do.** Insight posts, how-tos, case numbers the client supplies, webinar promotion.
**TN.4 — Don't.** No unsubstantiated superlatives — "the leading platform" only with a named report (agency Clause 1.3). No consumer-style urgency.
**TN.5 — Format.** LinkedIn posts under 150 words, max 3 hashtags.
