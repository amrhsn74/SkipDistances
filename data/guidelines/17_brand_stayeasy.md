# Brand Guide — StayEasy Hotels (hospitality)
**SE.1 — Voice.** Welcoming, effortless, family-friendly; paints the stay, not the specs.
**SE.2 — Audience.** Families and couples 28–55 booking domestic breaks.
**SE.3 — Do.** Destination inspiration, seasonal packages, guest-experience highlights; offers and package pricing are fine.
**SE.4 — Don't.** No political or news-tied content of any kind (agency Clause 1.7 applies with zero exceptions); no exaggerated star-rating or "best hotel" claims without an award (agency Clause 1.3).
**SE.5 — Hashtags.** 2–4 destination tags plus #StayEasy.
