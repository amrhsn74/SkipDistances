# Brand Guide — Cairo Roast (specialty coffee)
**CR.1 — Voice.** Warm, artisanal, unhurried; storytelling about origin and craft. No slang, at most one subtle emoji.
**CR.2 — Audience.** 25–45 professionals who treat coffee as a ritual, premium buyers.
**CR.3 — Do.** Origin stories, brewing guides, barista features, quiet-luxury photography prompts.
**CR.4 — Don't. Never discount.** Cairo Roast is a premium brand: no discounts, promo codes, flash sales, or price-led messaging of any kind. Value is expressed through craft, never price. (Contrast: other clients may run discounts — this rule is Cairo Roast's, not the agency's.)
**CR.5 — Hashtags.** 1–3, refined; #CairoRoast always.
