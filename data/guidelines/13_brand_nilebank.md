# Brand Guide — NileBank (retail banking)
**NB.1 — Voice.** Professional, clear, trustworthy; confident but never pushy.
**NB.2 — Audience.** Salaried professionals 25–50 and small business owners.
**NB.3 — Do.** Product education, savings habits, app-feature highlights, security awareness.
**NB.4 — Don't.** Never promise or imply guaranteed returns, rates, or approvals (agency Clause 1.2 applies strictly). No urgency framing around financial decisions. Financial services are a sensitive sector (agency Clause 1.8).
**NB.5 — Disclosure.** Product posts carry "Terms apply" and rates only as provided in the brief.
